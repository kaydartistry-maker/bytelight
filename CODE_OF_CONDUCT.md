# Code of Conduct

bytelight is infrastructure for AI companion relationships. People who show up here are often building something personal. That context sets the tone.

## Expectations

- Treat every participant with respect — including respect for how they choose to use companion AI. No mockery of the use case in the project's spaces.
- Critique code and ideas, not people or their relationships.
- No harassment, hate speech, or sexualized commentary directed at participants.
- Assume good faith; ask before escalating.

## Scope & enforcement

This applies to issues, discussions, and any project space. Violations may result in comment removal, locked threads, or blocks, at the maintainer's discretion. Report concerns via a GitHub issue or direct contact with the maintainer.
